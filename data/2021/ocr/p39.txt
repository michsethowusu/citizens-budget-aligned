Əman Sikasem Ntotoee Mu Nsakraec Dwumadie - PFMRD (Public
Financial Management Reforms Project): Saa sikasem ntotoee mu
nsakraes yi mpontuo botaee ne se Sikasem Nhyehysee ho ntotoee beko
yie, yebenya ahohyesoo wo sikasem ho anammontuo mu na yeahwe abo
Ghana Aban sika a əde di dwuma ho amanes wə ekwan pa so wo sbere
a ese mu. Saa sikasem ntotoes yi nam nhyehysee a edi mu ne sikasem
adwumayefoo potee bi nteteee so boa ma sikasem ho akwankyere so-die
tu mpon, boa ma yede sika di dwuma a shia mapa na afei, eboa ma yedi
yen dwuma yie ma manfo nya ho mfasov.

 

Sikasem Mpontuo a esi ani soə (Projected Growth Rate): Yei ye
sedes ani da ekwan se Ghana sikasem betu mpon wo afe potee bi mu.

(PFI): Private Finance Initiative.-

 

Sika A Aban Nya (Revenue): Yei ye sika dodos a Aban nya firi ne
dwumadie ahodos a ədi wo əman yi mu ne amannone.

 

Mmoayen A Ede Nnwuma Ba - RFJ (Rearing For Jobs): Yei ye Ghana
Aban mpontuo ho dwumadie foforo a əde aba a snam so bema
ntotemmoayen adwuma no adi mu na sika aba mu seedee gbeye a,
ntotemmoa nam bedəəso ama əmanfovs anya bi awe na yeate ntotemmoa
nam a yekra ba əman yi mu no so koraa, na enam so ama nnwuma abu
so a əmanfoo benya bi aye ama won a wədi ntotemmoa ho dwuma
nyinaa no asetenam nso atu mpon.

 

Asetena mu Mmoa Sika (Social Benefits): Yei ye sika a Aban gyina
manfo anan mu tua ma nsuo, petro ne nkanea nnwumakuo no de sə
Ghanafos asene.

 

Asenesə Sika (Subsidy): Yei ye sika mu mmoa bi a Aban de ma əman
mma ma eyi adesoa kakra firi won so ma won kon mu dwo won.
