21. Ghana Aban daa dwumadie bi a yato edin se Ghana COVID-19
Asetore Ne Ankorankore Nnwuma Mu Nkanyan ho Mmoa (Ghana
COVID-19 Alleviation and Revitalization of Enterprises Support-
Ghana CARES) no adi wo Obubuo bosome da a eto so 20 wə afe
2020 mu. Saa dwumadie yi botaes ne se ebebre yadee kontonkurowi
ho nsunsuansos no ase na aboa nnwuma ama wode nhyehyeees pa
adi dwuma ama wəatumi agyina won nan so ntem pa ara na enam so
ama Əman yi sikasem nso atu mpon. Nokwasem ne se, yei ye
akokoəduru kwan a yebetumi afa so asakra Ghanaman sikasem mu
ama agyina yie, ama mfidinnwuma aba, yiedie beba na Əman yi ade
ne ho nam so atumi aso Ghanafoo nyinaa ahiadee mapa ano ama
won.
