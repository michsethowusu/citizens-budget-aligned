 

Asentire: “Yebewie dwumadie ama ases atim na yzatoa des aka so nam so akanyan əman sikasem”

 

Dwumadie akyiridie (Surveillance): Yei ye kwan a ysfa so de ani di
biribi akyi na yehwe sedee esi tee.

 

Fannye (Transparency): Yei ne se Aban bema oman mma ahunu dee
greko so na woate amanesboə nyinaa wo ebere ano ebere ano.

 

Adekyere ne Adesua ho Akade: -TLM (Teaching And Learning
Materials): Yeinom ye nneema a nkwa wə mu ne dees nkwa nni mu anaa
se nnipa anaa abodes afoforo bi a əkyerekyereni de di dwuma wə
adekyere ne adesua mu sedes ebsye a sbeboa ama adekyere ne adesua
mu botaees no awie peye.

 

Adwade: ne Dwumadie ho sika (Use of Goods and Services): Yei ye
sika dodos a Aban tua wo dwuma a ədi ne dwumadie a ədi ma əmanfoo
no ho.
