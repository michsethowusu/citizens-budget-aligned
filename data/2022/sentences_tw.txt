COVID-19 yadeɛ kontonkurowi ho nsunsuansoɔ bɔne no aka ewiase ɔman biara sikasɛm a Ghanaman ka ho bi.
Ahohiahia a ɛkɔwiee owuo, asetenam ahokyere ne adwumasɛeɛ a Ghanafoɔ hunuiɛ no nyɛ adeɛ a Aban werɛ afiri koraa.
Mede ahohoahoa ka sɛ anammɔn a ɔman yi tuu wɔ yadeɛ kontonkurowi yi ho no yɛ akokoɔduru anammɔn na ɛyɛ gyinaeɛsie a ɛdi mu pa ara a ewiase aman nyinaa agye atom sɛ yɛayɛ adeɛ.
Aban sane yɛɛ nhyehyɛeɛ ahodoɔ nam so boaa ankorankorɛ nnwuma sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a ɔmanfoɔ bɛsane asi wɔn dada mu.
Deɛ ɛdi hɔ bio nso ne sɛ, sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ na yɛbɛtumi agyina ɔhaw ne akwannya ahodoɔ a yadeɛ kontonkurowi yi de aba no ano enti, Aban nam Ɔmanpanin Nana Addo Ghana Dankwa Akufo-Addo mpaninnie a ɛdi mu no so de ahoɔherɛ hyehyɛɛ Covid- (GhanaCARES) 19 Asetɔre Ne Ankorankorɛ Nnwuma Mu Nkanyan Ho Mmoa "Obaatan Pa- Dwumadie (Ghana COVID-19 Alleviation and Revitalisation of Enterprises programme, (the GhanaCARES “Obaatan Pa” programme) a emu (GH¢100 billion) sikabotene yɛ sidi ɔpepepem ɔha baako .
Yei de ahomegyeɛ mapa mu. abrɛ yɛn nkurɔfoɔ na ɛgu so de nkanyan reba ɔman no ɔyɛ yɛn ahomeka sɛ mogu so wɔ gyidie wɔ Aban mu wɔ ɛberɛ a yɛgu so reyɛ biribiara a ɛhia de ama yɛade yɛ ho afiri yadeɛ kontonkurowi yi ho. ɔnam deɛ ɛtoo yɛn wɔ saa ɛberɛ no mu no so ama yɛahunu sɛdeɛ yɛn nsiyɛ sɛ ɔman a yɛtumi gyina atoyerɛnkyɛm ano no teɛ.
Twerɛ Kronkron no ma yɛn akwankyerɛ ɛberɛ biara a ɛsɛ sɛ yɛfa so wɔ mmerɛ a ɛte sei mu wɔ Yesaia nwoma, etire 9, nkyekyɛmu 10 hɔ a wɔatwerɛ sɛ, “Berekese no adwiri agu fam, nanso yɛde aboɔ a wɔatwa bɛto bio; wɔatwitwa borɔdɔma nnua no Saa ara na Aban asi nkete sɛ ɔbɛdi ne agu nanso yɛde ntweneduro bɛhyɛ anan mu.” bɔhyɛ nyinaa so na ne saa enti, ɔbɛkɔ so ayɛ nhyehyɛeɛ na wadi dwuma ahodoɔ a ɛbɛma yɛn nsa aka deɛ yɛahwere nyinaa na ɛnam so ama ɔmanfoɔ asetenam atu mpɔn.
Botaeɛ ahodoɔ no mu baako a ɛdi akotene wɔ 2022 Sikasɛm Nhyehyɛeɛ yi mu ɛnna afei wɔ mfeɛ mmiɛnsa a ɛdi yɛn anim yi mu ne sɛ, yɛbɛkyekyere Ɔman a Ankorankorɛ Nnwumabue ase bɛtim wɔ mu na ɛbɛma yɛn sikasɛm kankorɔ adi mu, na obiara bɛdi ne dwuma sɛdeɛ ɛfata na ɛnam so ama nnwuma abu so na ne titire, mmabunu anya bi ayɛ.
Yei enti, Aban nhyehyɛeɛ a ɔbɛtu ho anammɔn no bɛyɛ deɛ ɛbɛboa mmabunu, nam so ahunu adom akyɛdeɛ a wɔwɔ na yɛaboa ama obiara anya akwannya de adi dwuma sɛdeɛ ɛbɛboa ama ahonya aba ama obiara.
Sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ na yɛbɛma mmabunu anya nkuranhyɛ na wɔanya nnnwumabue sunsum de abuebue wɔn ankasa nnwuma enti, afe 2022 mu no, Aban bɛhyɛ dwumadie bi a YouStart yɛato edin Nhyehyɛeɛ ase a ɛnam so bɛma mmabunu anya bosea de ahyɛ aseɛ abuebue wɔn nnwuma na atumi agyina anaa wɔde atrɛ wɔn nnwuma mu.
Afe 2022 Sikasɛm Nhyehyɛeɛ yi mu no, yɛayɛ nhyehyɛeɛ ahodoɔ a yɛde bɛtoa so atu anammɔn wɔ ɔman yi mu ɔhaw ahodoɔ a yɛwɔ mu seesei no ho nam so ama yɛatumi agyina yɛn nan so bio wɔ yadeɛ kontonkurowi yi akyi.
Aban de asi n’ani so sɛ ɔbɛyɛ ntotoeɛ a ɛdi mu na ɔne akuhodoɔ adi nsawɔsoɔ de atu anammɔn a ɛbɛma mmabunu anya nsaanonnwuma ho nteteeɛ na yɛahwɛ ama ankorankorɛ nnwumabue atu mpɔn wɔ mmabunu mu. ɔberɛ a yɛgu so redi dwuma wɔ Ɔmanpanin anisoadehunu a ɔpɛ sɛ Ghana duru mpɛmpɛnsoɔ a yɛrenhia mmoa (Ghana Beyond Aid) ho no, mɛsrɛ Ghanafoɔ nyinaa, titire mmabubu no sɛ wɔnhunu sɛ wɔwɔ kyɛfa wɔ ɔman yi mu, wɔnsusu biribi pa na wɔmfa wɔn ho nhyɛ ankorankorɛ nnwumabue ɔman a yɛrekyekyere ama aseɛ atim no mu na ɛnwie pɛyɛ.
Mehyɛ yɛn nyinaa nkuran sɛ yɛmmoa na yɛnkyekyere Ghanaman a yɛn nyinaa hwehwɛ no.

Afe 2022 Ɔman Mma Sikasɛm Nhyehyɛeɛ yi yɛ dwuma a Aban dii wɔ 2021 mu no ho tɔfabɔ ne sika dodoɔ a Aban hwɛ anim sɛ ɔbɛnya de atu ne nhyehyɛeɛ ne ne dwumadie ahodoɔ ho anammɔn wɔ afe 2022 mu ne mmerɛ kakra a ɛdi yɛn anim yi mu.
Sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ na anammɔn a yɛretu ama asetenam ne sikasɛm mpontuo asane apagya na ɔman mpontuo botaeɛ ahodoɔ abam no enti, afe 2022 Sikasɛm Nhyehyɛeɛ yi botaeɛ ne sɛ yɛbɛfa Sikasɛm Ntotoeɛ pa so bɛkyekyere Ɔman a Ankorankorɛ Nnwumabue ase bɛtim wɔ mu na Nnwuma abu so ama ɔman mma anya bi ayɛ.

Sika A Ɛbaa Ɔman Fotoɔ Mu 4. Sika a Aban gyegyee ne deɛ wɔde kyɛɛ ɔman no firi Ɔpɛpɔn kɔsi Ɛbɔ bosome mu wɔ afe 2021 mu no nyinaa kɔsii ɔpepepem aduanan nsia ne ɔpepem aduɔwɔtwe enum (GH¢46.85 billion) a ɛyɛ ɔman mu adwadeɛ nyinaa mu ɔha mu nkyɛmu edu ne akyire pɔ nson (10.7% of GDP), na yɛyɛɛ dwumadie mu nsakraeɛ no, na sika dodoɔ a yɛde yɛn ani asi so sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ yɛnya no yɛ ɔpepepem aduonum baako ne ɔpepem aduasa baako (GH¢51.31 billion) a ɛyɛ ɔman mu adwadeɛ nyinaa mu ɔha mu nkyɛmu dubaako ne akyire pɔ nson (11.7% of GDP).
Sika dodoɔ a ɛbaa ɔman fotoɔ mu no na ɛdidi soɔ yi: Ɛtoɔ a ɛmfiri fango dwumadie ho sika mu (Non-oil Tax Revenue) a ɛyɛ ɔha mu nkyɛmu aduoson ɛnan (74%), sika a ɛmfiri fango mu na ɛnyɛ ɛtoɔ sika (Nonoil-non-Tax Revenue) a ɛyɛ ɔha mu nkyɛmu nkron (9%), sika a ɛfiri Fango ne Ogyaframa mu (Oil and Gas Receipts) a ɛyɛ ɔha mu nkyɛmu nkron (9%), sika afoforɔ (other Revenue) a ɛyɛ ɔha mu nkyɛmu nsia (6%) ɛnna sika a wɔde kyɛɛ ɔman no (Grants) a ɛyɛ ɔha mu nkyɛmu mmienu (2%) sɛdeɛ yɛada no adi wɔ Hyehyɛpono 1 mu no.
Afe 2021 Sika dodoɔ a Aban gyegyee no annuru deɛ yɛde sii yɛn ani so sɛ yɛn nsa bɛka no ɛsiane sɛ Nnwumakuo Mfasoɔ ho Ɛtoɔ (Corporate Income Tax), Ankorankorɛ Sika ho Ɛtoɔ (Personal Income Tax), Fagudeɛ ho Ɛtoɔ, Ɔman yi mu Adwadeɛ ho Ɛtoɔ (Domestic Excise Duty) ne Nkratoɔ Dwumadie ho Ɛtoɔ (Communications Service Tax) nyinaa so teeɛ.
Dwuma A Yɛde Sika Diiɛ 6. Ɛbɔ bosome kɔɔ awieeɛ wɔ afe 2021 mu no, na Sika dodoɔ a Aban de adi dwuma (a ɛka a yɛatua ka ho bi) no aduru Ghana sika sidi ɔpepepem aduɔwɔtwe ne ɔpepem aduoson baako (GH¢80.71 billion) a ɛyɛ ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ mu ɔha mu nkyɛmu dunwɔtwe ne akyire pɔ ɛnan (18.4% of GDP) a wode toto dwumadie mu nsakraeɛ a yɛyɛɛ no ho a, na ɛsɛ sɛ anka yɛde Ghana sika sidi ɔpepepem aduɔwɔtwe mmiɛnsa ne ɔpepem aduɔwɔtwe nkron (GH¢83.89 billion) a ɛyɛ ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ mu ɔha mu nkyɛmu dunkron ne akyire pɔ baako (19.1% of GDP) na ɛdi dwuma.
Adwumayɛfoɔ akatua ne Nsiho-tua na na ɛyɛ nneɛma mmienu a Aban bɔɔ ho ɛka pa ara.
Sikasɛm Nhyehyɛeɛ ho Anammɔntuo ne Dwumadie ho Sikapɛ 7. Sika dodoɔ a Aban de dii dwuma boroo so ma ɛka a ɛbaa so yɛ ɔpepepem aduasa mmiɛnsa ne ɔpepem aduɔwɔtwe nsia (GH¢33.86 billion) a ɛyɛ ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ mu ɔha mu nkyɛmu nson ne akyire pɔ nson (7.7% of GDP) a na anka ɛsɛ sɛ ɛyɛ ɔpepepem aduasa mmienu ne ɔpepem aduonum nwɔtwe (GH¢32.58 billion) a ɛyɛ ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ mu ɔha mu nkyɛmu nson ne akyire pɔ ɛnan (7.4% of GDP).
Aban pɛɛ ɛka a ɛboroo so yi ho sika firii ɔman yi mu ne amannɔne.
Sika dodoɔ a Aban nyaa no wɔ ɔman yi mu no kɔduruu ɔpepepem aduonu ne ɔpepem aduasa nkron (GH¢20.39 billion) a ɛyɛ ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ ɔha mu nkyɛmu aduosia ne akyire pɔ mmienu (60.2% of GDP) na sika a Aban nya firii amannɔne nso kɔduruu ɔpepepem dumiɛnsa ne ɔpepem aduanan nson (GH¢13.47 billion) a ɛyɛ ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ ɔha mu
Nkyɛmu aduasa nkron (39.8% of GDP).
Ɛka A Ɔman Bɔɔ Wɔ afe 2021 Mu 8. Amaneɛbɔ a ɛwɔ hɔ seesei ne sɛ, Ɛbɔ bosome baa awieeɛ wɔ afe 2021 mu no, na ɛka a yɛabɔ yɛ Ghana sika ɔpepepem ahasa aduanan baako ne ɔpepem aduɔson nsia (GH¢341.76 billion) a wode kɔ Amɛrika sika mu a, ɛyɛ dɔla ɔpepepem aduonum nwɔtwe ne ɔpepem ahasa ne aduanan nwɔtwe (US$58.348 billion).
Yei gyina hɔ ma ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ mu ɔha mu nkyɛmu aduɔson nson ne akyire pɔ nwɔtwe (77.8 % of GDP).
Deɛ ɛka bom yɛ ɛka a ɔman yi de no ne amannɔne ɛka a ɛyɛ Ghana sika ɔpepepem ɔha aduosia mmiɛnsa ne ɔpepem aduosia enum (GH¢163.65 billion) a wode kɔ Amɛrika sika mu a, ɛyɛ dɔla ɔpepepem aduonu nson ne ɔpepem aduɔwɔtwe nwɔtwe (US$27.88 billion) a ɛyɛ ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ ɔha mu nkyɛmu aduanan nson ne akyire pɔ nkron (47.9% of GDP) ɛnna afei ɔman yi mu ɛka a ɛno nso yɛ Ghana sika ɔpepepem ɔha aduɔson nwɔtwe ne ɔpepem dubaako (GH¢178.11 billion) a wode kɔ Amɛrika sika mu a, ɛyɛ dɔla ɔpepepem aduasa ne ɔpepem aduasa enum (US$30.35 billion) a ɛyɛ ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ ɔha mu nkyɛmu aduonum mmienu ne akyire pɔ baako (52.1 % of GDP).
Nneɛma a ɛma Ɔman sikasɛm gyina a yɛde ani abu sɛ yɛbɛnya wɔ 2022 mu 9. Ɔman sikasɛm nnyinasoɔ a ɛddi soɔ yi na yɛde yɛn ani abu sɛ yɛn nsa bɛka wɔ afe 2022 mu: Ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ nyinaa kankorɔ ɔha mu nkyɛmu enum ne akyire pɔ nwɔtwe (Real GDP growth of 5.8 %), Ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ a Fango nka ho bie kankorɔ ɔha mu nkyɛmu enum akyire pɔ nkron (Non-Oil Real GDP growth of 5.9 %), Ɔpɛnimma bosome awieeɛ nneɛma boɔ-sorokɔ ɔha mu nkyɛmu nwɔtwe (End-December inflation of 8 %), Sika a yɛbɛdi aboro deɛ yɛwɔ so- ɔha mu nkyɛmu nson ne akyire pɔ ɛnan (Fiscal deficit of 7.4 % of GDP), Sika dodoɔ a ɛbɛboro deɛ yɛbɛgyegyeɛ so- ɔha mu nkyɛmu hwee ne akyire pɔ baako (Primary surplus of 0.1 % of GDP), ɛnna Amannɔne Sika Dodoɔ a ɛsɛ sɛ yɛnya de kra nneɛma bɛyɛ abosome ɛnan.
Ɛkwan a yɛbɛfa so apɛ sika wɔ 10. Aban ani da ɛkwan sɛ sika dodoɔ a ɔbɛtumi agyegye ne deɛ wɔde bɛkyɛ yɛn wɔ afe 2022 mu no nyinaa bɛduru Ghana sika sidi ɔpepepem ɔha ne ɔpepem mmienu (GH¢100.52 billion) a ɛyɛ ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ mu ɔha mu aduonum nkyɛmu aduonu ne akyire pɔ (20.0 % of GDP).
Sika dodoɔ a yɛde ani hwee abu sɛ yɛn nsa bɛka wɔ ɔman yi ara mu no yɛ ɔpepepem aduɔkron nkron ne ɔpepem aduonum enum (GH¢99.55 billion).
Sika a yɛbɛnya wɔ ɔman yi ara mu no, ɛtoɔ sika a ɛmfiri fango mu yɛ ɔha mu nkyɛmu aduɔson nson ne akyire pɔ enum (77.5 %) a ɛyɛ Ghana sika ɔpepem aduɔson nson ne mpem ɔha ne aduasa nsia (GH¢77,136 million).
Aban ani da ɛkwan sɛ sika dodoɔ a yɛbɛgyegye no bɛgyina ɛtoɔtua ho nokorɛdie, sikagyegyeɛ ho nhyehyɛeɛ mu nsakraeɛ ne ɛtoɔtua nhyehyɛeɛ ho ntotoeɛ pa so.

Sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ na yɛbɛduru yɛn botaeɛ a yɛpɛ sɛ Ghana duru mpɛmpɛnsoɔ a yɛrenhia mmoa ho, na afei yɛasane agyina yɛn nan so wɔ COVID-19 Yadeɛ Kontonkurowi akyi enti, Ghana Aban hyɛɛ aseɛ tuu Ghana Covid-19 Asetɔre (GhanaCARES) "Obaatan Ne Ankorankorɛ Nnwuma Mu Nkanyan Ho Mmoa Pa- (GH¢100 Dwumadie a emu sikabotene yɛ sidi ɔpepepem ɔha baako billion) ho anammɔn.

Deɛ ɛdidi soɔ yi ne dwumadie no mu nkonimdie ahodoɔ a epuee wɔ afe 2021 mu ɛnna afei anammɔn ahodoɔ a yɛasi gyinaeɛ sɛ yɛbɛtu wɔ afe 2022 mu.
Abɛɛfo Ɛkwan a Yɛfa so yɛ Kua ne Nnuanekra Nhyɛananmu 19. Ahineme bosome baa awieeɛ wɔ afe 2021 mu no, na yɛafa abɛɛfo ɛkwan so atwerɛ nnipa dodoɔ mpem ahanum aduɔson nkron, ahansia aduosia mmienu (579,662 individuals) edin a emu mpem ahanum aduanan nson, ɔha ne aduonu nsia yɛ akuafoɔ (547,126 farmers) ɛnna emu mpem aduasa mmienu, ahanum ne aduasa nsia yɛ nnipa a wɔdi dwuma ahodoɔ wɔ kua mu (32,536 value chain actors) na yei bɛboa ama yɛatumi de yɛn ani adi wɔn dwumadie akyi, ahunu obiara dwuma a ɔredi sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a, Aban mmoa a ɔde boa kuadwuma no bɛdi mu na emu ayɛ fann a obiara bɛhunu deɛ ɛrekɔ soɔ.

Sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ na abɛɛfo kuadwuma a yɛahyɛ aseɛ yi ase bɛtim enti, yɛbɛtu anammɔn ahodoɔ a ɛdidi soɔ yi wɔ afe 2022 mu: yɛbɛbue Ghana Kuadwuma Nsase ho Amaneɛbɔ Beaeɛ (Ghana Agricultural Land Information Bank-GhLIB) a ɛbɛboa ama wɔn a wɔrepɛ asase ayɛ kuadwuma atɔn mu nnɔbaeɛ no anya bi, yɛbɛfa abɛɛfo asetenam nkutahodie ne nkratoɔ akwanhodoɔ (social media) so ama mmabunu nkyerɛkyerɛ na yɛne wɔn anya nkutahodie afa akwannya ahodoɔ a ɛwɔ kuadwuma mu no ho, na afei yɛbɛyɛ nhwehwɛmu afa kuadwuma mu edwadie ho nam so ahunu nhyehyɛeɛ ne akadeɛ a ɛbɛboa saa kuadwuma mu edwadie yi na yɛatumi de akadeɛ ne mmoa ama mmabunu.

Sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ na yɛbɛdi nnuatwa ne kwaeɛsɛe ho ɔhaw so no enti, Ɔmanpanin dii ɔmanfoɔ anim ma yɛduaduaa nnua boro mpem nson wɔ Ayɛwohomumɔ wɔ afe 2021 mu.
Yei boroo nnua mpem enum a na ɛsi ani so sɛ yɛbɛduadua wɔ nhyehyɛeɛ a wɔato edin Green Ghana no mu no so.
Afe 2022-2025 Animanim yi ara mu Ɔman Mpontuo Nhyehyɛeɛ ( Medium- Term National Development Policy Framework (MTNDPF) no na ɛbɛsii nhyehyɛeɛ a wɔato edin sɛ Ghanaman yi mu animanim yi mpontuo botaeɛ (2018-2021) (Ghanaqs Medium-Term National Development Policy Framework -2018-2021) no anan mu.
Mpontuo ho nhyehyɛeɛ ahodoɔ a ɛdidi soɔ yi ne Nhyehyɛeɛ foforɔ yi nnyinasoɔ: Ghanaman yi Daakye mu Mpontuo Botaeɛ (Long-Term National Development Framework - Ghanal100), Sikasɛm ne Asetenam Mpontuo Nhyehyɛeɛ Ahodoɔ (Coordinated Programme of Economic and Social Development Policies (2017-2024), Mpɛmpɛnsoɔ a Ghana nhia GhanaCOVID-19 asetɔre ne ankorankorɛ mmoa, (Ghana Beyond Aid) ɛnna nnwuma nkanyan ho mmoa (GhanaCARES).
Saa nhyehyɛeɛ yi botaeɛ foforɔ ne sɛ ɛbɛma yɛatumi aduru Amanaman Nkabom Ekuo nhyehyɛeɛ a wɔato edin "Agenda 2030- (SDGs) ne Abibirem Nkabom Botaeɛ a wɔato edin "Africa Union Agenda 2063- no ho.
Saa ɔfa yi da dwuma a Aban adi wɔ ɔman yi asoɛeɛ ahodoɔ no mu wɔ afe 2021 mu ne dwuma a ɔbɛdi wɔ afe 2022 mu no adi.
Sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ na Aban bɛtumi ama ɔmanfoɔ anya nnwuma ayɛ enti no, ɔde nhyehyɛeɛ ne dwuma ahodoɔ bi te sɛ "Ɔmanfoɔ Adwumanya Nhyehyɛeɛ (Ghana Productive Safety Net Project- (GPSNP) agu akwan mu.
Dwumadie yi mu no, Aban: i. maa ɔmanfoɔ a wɔwɔ Nkuropɔn, Nkuro, ne Mansini, aduɔwɔtwe (80 MMDAs) a wɔyɛ ahiafoɔ pa ara mu dodoɔ mpem aduasa mmiɛnsa aha nkron aduonum mmienu (33,952 extreme poor persons) nnwuma yɛeɛ na wɔn ho akatua nyinaa dodoɔ som sika ɔpepem aduonum mmiɛnsa ne mpem aduanan mmiɛnsa (GH¢53.43 million total wage payout), ii.
Kyekyɛɛ sika dodoɔ ɔpepem enum ne mpem aduɔwɔtwe mmienu GH¢5.82) maa fidua ahodoɔ ɔpepem nkron ahanu ne adunan ɛnan (9,244) a wɔwɔ mpɔtam ahodoɔ ɔha ne aduonu nwɔtwe mu (128 communities) wɔ Nkuropɔn, Nkuro ne Mansini aduanan baako (41 MMDAs) a ɛwɔ Atifi Amantam enum mu sɛ wɔmfa nhyɛ nnwuma ase anaa wɔmfa ntrɛ wɔn nnwuma no mu.
Aban nhyehyɛeɛ a ɛwɔ hɔ sɛ ɔbɛma mmeranteɛ ne mmabaawa adi dwuma ahodoɔ, nam so de ate kuro kɛseɛm atukɔtena (rural-urban) so, na ama wɔn asetena mu atu mpɔn enti no, ɔnam nnuadua, kookoo mfuo so adupeteɛ, ɔyɛasaseyie-peteɛ, nnuabaguo, kookoo nnua mman a yadeɛ aka no ho twitwa ne kookoo mfuo-so-nsusuguo so maa nnipa dodoɔ mpem ɔha ne aduasa nyaa nnwuma yɛeɛ (130,000 jobs were created).
Ɔman Kwaeɛ mu Nnua Nhyɛananmu Dwumadie (National Afforestation Programme -NAP) no maa akwannya ma wɔfaa nnipa dodoɔ mpem aduɔson mmienu (72,000 persons) adwumam.
Nnipa dodoɔ yi boroo nnipa mpem aduosia (60, 000 persons) a na ɛsi yɛn ani so sɛ yɛbɛfa wɔn adwumam ama wɔaboa nnuadua dwumadie ahodoɔ no.
Yɛgyina Mmabunu Kwaeɛm Nnua Nhyɛananmu Dwumadie (Youth in Afforestation Programme) no so faa nnipa no mu dodoɔ mpem aduasa enum (35,000) ɛnna yɛgyinaa deɛ wɔato edin sɛ Modified Taungya System (MTS) no so faa nnipa no mu dodoɔ mpem Dunson (17,000) ɛnna afei yɛgyinaa dwumadie a wɔfa nnipa ma wɔgu aba so faa nnipa mpem mmienu (2,000).

Mmabunu Adwumanya Adwumakuo (Youth Employment Agency (YEA) no gyinaa wɔn nkorabata ahodoɔ so faa nnipa dodoɔ mpem aduɔwɔtwe mmienu, ahanwɔtwe ne aduosia nkron (82,869 beneficiaries).
Beaeɛ a wɔfrɛ no "The Job Centre-, a ɛyɛ ntamgyinafoɔ ma wɔn a wɔrepɛ adwuma ne nnwumakuo no boa maa nnipa dodoɔ apem baako, ɔha ne aduɔwɔtwe enum (1,185 persons) nyaa adwuma.
Ɔhaw a COVID-19 yareɛ no de baeɛ no nyinaa akyi, yɛgu so ara renya kankorɔ wɔ Mansini Baako Afididwuma Baako (IDIF) ho dwumadie mu.
Ɛfiri afe 2017 bɛsi ɛnnɛ, Mansini Baako Afididwuma Baako(1D1F) dodoɔ ahanu aduoson nwɔtwe (278 1D1F) na yɛadi ɛho dwuma ama aduru mpɛmpɛnsoɔ ahodoɔ.
Emu ɔha ne nsia gu so redi dwuma seesei (106 in operation), yɛgu so a yɛresi emu ɔha ne aduanan nwɔtwe (148 construction), ɛnna emu aduonu ɛnan deɛ, afei koraa na wɔrebɛfiri aseɛ (24 pipeline projects).
Bɛsi ɛnnɛ nnwuma no a wɔafiri aseɛ redi dwuma no afa nnipa ahodoɔ mpem ɔha aduonum nsia, ahanson aduɔwɔtwe mmienu (156,782 persons) wɔ adwumam) na saa nnipa dodoɔ yi mpem dunson, ahanan ne aduonum baako (17,451 persons) deɛ, wɔfaa wɔn wɔ adwuma mu wɔ afe 2021 mu.

Saa nnwumakuo yi mu biara wɔ nnipa dodoɔ bɛyɛ aduanan kɔsi aduanan nwɔtwe a wɔde wɔn sika ahyɛ nnwuma no mu.
Mansini Baako Afididwuma Baako (1D1F) ahodoɔ ahanu aduɔson nwɔtwe (278 1D1F) a ɛwɔ mantam ahodoɔ no mu na ɛdidi soɔ yi: 1D1F dodoɔ a ɛwɔ Mantam ahodoɔ no mu Ahafo Mantam 5 Asante Mantam 54 Bono Apueeɛ Mantam 10 Bono Mantam 19 Mfinimfini Mantam 19 Apueeɛ Mantam 37 Nkran Mantam 76 Atifi Apueeɛ Mantam 3 Atifi Mantam 6 Oti Mantam 3 Savanna Mantam (Ɛserɛm) 4 Atifi Apueɛ Mantam 7 Atifi Atɔeɛ Mantam 6 Firaw Mantam 13 Atɔeɛ Mantam 11 Atɔeɛ Atifi mantam 5

Afe 2022 mu no, Mmabunu Adwumanya Adwumakuo no (YEA) no ne wɔn a wɔanya adwuma dodoɔ mpem ɔha ne nsia (106,000 beneficiaries) wɔ 1D1F mu no bɛnya nsawɔsodie.
Ahyɛn Akwaa-hyehyɛ ne Ahyɛn ho Afididwuma (Vehicle Assembly and Automotive Industry) a ɛyɛ Ghana Ahyɛn ho Afididwuma Mpontuo dwumadie no fa bi boa maa Toyota Tsusho Aafididwuma no, buee Ahyɛn Akwaa-hyehyɛ Adwumakuo wɔ Ayɛwohomumɔ bosome mu wɔ afe 2021 mu nam so firii aseɛ hyehyɛɛ Toyota ne Suzuki ahyɛn – Hilux Pickup ne Suzuki Swift – wɔ Ghana.
Volkswagen firii ahyɛn dodoɔ yɛ ase wɔ Ɔsanaa bosome mu wɔ afe 2020 mu wɔ Ghanaman mu na wɔtoaa so wɔ afe 2021 mu.
Kantanka Ɛhyɛn ho adwumakuo no nso pagyaa wɔn ahyɛn a wɔyɛ wɔ Ghanaman mu ha no ho dwumadie mu.
Afe 2022 mu no ani da ɛkwan sɛ Ahyɛn Akwaa-hyehyɛ Nnwumakuo ɛnan a wɔn ne Nissan, KIA, Hyundai ne Renault nso bɛfiri aseɛ ayɛ ahyɛn no dodoɔ a wɔbɛtɔn.
Ɛsiane Ɔmanpanin anisoadehunu a ɛfa nnuadua a wɔde ma ɔman mu yɛ fɛ ho no enti, Adwumakuo a ɛhwɛ Pramma ne Turo ahodoɔ so (Department of Parks and Gardens) duaa nnua dodoɔ mpem edu ahanu ne nkron (10,209 trees) wɔ ɔman yi afanan nyinaa mu de hyɛɛ Ewiase Atenaeɛ Da afahyɛ no.
Ɔman Kwaeɛ Nhyɛananmu dwumadie no mu no, yɛde sii yɛn ani so sɛ yɛbɛdua nnua wɔ asase hekta mpem aduɔwɔtwe (80,000 ha) so, nanso yɛtumi duaa nnua no wɔ asase hɛkta mpem aduɔson enum, ɔha aduɔson (75,170ha) so, na yei mu no, yɛduaa nnua nketewa wɔ asase hɛkta mpem dunan, ne aduonum nson a ɛyɛ kwaeɛ foforɔ (14,057 new forest plantations) so, afei, asase hɛkta mpem edu, ahanan aduokron enum a ɛso nnua anyɛ yie (10,495 ha) no nso yɛduaa nnua hyɛɛ anan mu, ɛnna yɛsane duaa nnua wɔ mfusase hɛkta mpem aduonum, ahansia ne dunwɔtwe (50,618 ha farm lands) so.
Bio yɛduaa nnua dodoɔ ɔpepem mmiɛnsa ne mpem nson (3.7million) a Ofram, Emire, Wawa, Kokrodua, Teak, Cedrela, Gmelina, Cassia, Eucalyptus ne Mahogany fra mu bie.
Ɛnam dwumadie a wɔato edin sɛ "Galamstop- no so ama asutene akɛseɛ bi te sɛ, Asuo Pra a ɛwɔ Twifo Praso, Asuo Pra a ɛwɔ Daboase, Asuo Ankobra a ɛwɔ Bonsaso, Asuo Tano a ɛwɔ Elubo, Asuo Birim a ɛwɔ Osino ne Asuo Birim a ɛwɔ Bunso nyinaa ani ate nwann.
Yɛhyɛɛ beaeɛ a wɔgya to hɔ de bɔ atenaeɛ ho ban ase wɔ mpɔtam ahodoɔ bi mu wɔ Firaw tadeɛ (Black and White Volta), Tano, Offin ne Densu, dii dwuma ahodoɔ pii a wɔnam so tetee ntutim ho nnipa dodoɔ ahanu aduonum mmienua (252 nursery attendants) wɔ mpɔtam nson mu, na afei yɛduaduaa nnua nketewa mpem dunsia, ahankron ne aduasa nson (16,937 seedlings).
Yei nyinaa botaeɛ ne sɛ ɛbɛboa ahyɛ atenaeɛ a agyigya wɔ asuo ho no na ate ɔhaw a nnipa dwumadie de aba asuo ahodoɔ no ho no so.
Yɛmaa ɔmanfoɔ gyaee sumina a wɔgugu wɔ Kpone (Nkran mantam no) ne Oti (Ashanti mantam) no na yɛkɔɔ so dii dwuma de nsakraeɛ baa nkuropɔn sumina ho ntotoeɛ mu ne mpɔtam ahodoɔ a ɛwɔ saa mmeaeɛ yi no atenaeɛ tebea mu.
Afe 2022 mu no, Pramma ne Turo Adwumakuo (Parks and Gardens Department) no bɛgu nnua aba dodoɔ mpem ahanan (400,000 seedlings) atɔn, na wɔagyina nnuadua ho dawubɔ dwumadie a wɔde ko tia ewiem nsesaeɛ ho ɔhaw no so adua nnua no dodoɔ mpem ɔha (100,000 trees) wɔ ɔman yi afanan nyinaa.
Ɛsiane Aban dwumadie a wato edin sɛ "Water for All- a yɛnam so bɛma obiara anya nsuo no enti, yɛahyɛ nsuo ho dwumadie ahodoɔ ase a emu biara aduru mpɛmpɛnsoɔ pɔtee bi.
Yeinom na ɛdidi soɔ yi: Atifi Apueeɛ Mantam nsuo ho dwumadie ɔfa a ɛdi kan (a nnipa a wɔwɔ Navrongo, Bolgatanga, Paga, Bongo ne ne mpɔtam bɛnya ho mfasoɔ) – wɔadi ɛho dwuma akɔduru ɔha mu nkyɛmu aduɔkron nwɔtwe (98 % completed) na wɔbɛpa ɛho ntoma wɔ Ɔpɛnimma bosome mu wɔ afe 2021 mu, Tamale Nsuo Dwumadie – yɛawie ɛho ntotoeɛ a ɛho dwumadie rebɛfiri aseɛ, Damango Nsuo Dwumadie – ɛho adrɔɔdrɔɔ ne nhyehyɛeɛ nyinaa adi mu a ɛrebɛfiri aseɛ, Wenchi Nsuo Dwumadie – yɛawie ɛho nhyehyɛeɛ nyinaa ama dwumadie no afiri aseɛ, Keta Nsuo Dwumadie – yɛawie ɛho nhyehyɛeɛ nyinaa ama dwumadie no gu so., ɛnna Sekondi-Takoradi Nsuo Dwumadie – yɛawie ɛho dwumadie nyinaa de nsa ahyehyɛ ɛho kɔntraagye ase.
Ɛduruu Ɛbɔ bosome da a ɛtɔ so aduasa wɔ afe 2021 mu no, na wɔatwerɛ Ghanafoɔ dodoɔ ɔpepem dunum, mpem ahansia aduonum nsia, ɔha ne aduosia (15,656,160 Ghanaians) edin wɔ Ɔmanbayɛ ho Adansedie Nwoma (National Identity Register (NIR) no mu, na wɔmaa saa nnipa yi mu dodoɔ ɔpepem dubaako, mpem ahansia aduanan nwɔtwe ne ahasa aduɔkron baako (11,648,391 Ghanaians) a na wɔadi boro mfeɛ dunum nyinaa Ɔman Kaade no bi (Ghana Cards).
Bio, yɛtwerɛɛ amannɔnefoɔ dodoɔ mpem ɔha aduosia mmiɛnsa ne ahansia aduɔkron enum (163,695 foreigners) edin memaa wɔn ahɔhoyɛ ho adansedie kaade (Non-Citizen Identity Cards).
Wɔfirii Ɔman SIM kaade ho edintwerɛ nhyehyɛeɛ no ase wɔ Ahinime bosome ɛda a ɛdi kan wɔ 2021 mu wɔ nɛtwork ahodoɔ no nyinaa so, na ɛbɛboa asi SIM kaade ho korɔnobɔ ne "mobael- fon a wɔde di dwuma a ɛmfa mmarakwan so no ano, na saa dwumadie yi bɛba awieeɛ wɔ Ɔbɛnem bosome awieeɛ wɔ 2022 mu.
Ɛduruu Ɛbɔ bosome awieeɛ wɔ afe 2021 mu no, na yɛayɛ ɛberɛ ano ɛberɛ ano nsiesie wɔ lɔre akwan a ɛka nkuro akɛseɛ bom kwansini mpem aduonu baako, ahansia ne enum (21,165 km), deɛ ɛkɔ beaeɛ a mfudeɛ firi ba kwansini mpem nsia, ahanu aduosia enum (6,265 km) ne kurom akwan kwansini mpem mmienu, ahankron ne aduasa nson (2,937 km).
Kutawonsa da a ɛtɔ so Nkron wɔ afe 2021 mu no, yɛpaa Pokuase lɔre ɛkwan abɔnta nsamsoɔ ho dwumadie a abɔntanan nsamsoɔ, lɔre akwan bi a wɔahyɛda ayiyi ato hɔ kwansini dummienu, beaeɛ a ɔmanfoɔ fa so wɔ lɔrekwan ho, ɛtwene a nnipa fa so, ɛtwene a nnipa fa aseɛ ne suka ahodoɔ ka ho no ho ntoma.
Afe 2022 mu no, yɛbɛyɛ nsiesie a emu bi ne lɔre akwan-so-dwie, lɔre akwan so ntokurotua,lɔre nkwankyɛn nsiesiene, lɔre ɛkwan ho nnuadua wɔ ɛkwan a ɛka nkuro akɛseɛ bɔ mu kwansini mpem aduonu enum (25,000km) deɛ ɛkɔ mmeaeɛ a mfudeɛ firi ba kwansini mpem dunsia(16,000km), ne kurom akwan kwansini mpem mmiɛnsa ahanum (3,500km)
Bio, yɛbɛyɛ ɛberɛ ano ɛberɛ ano nsiesie dwumadie wɔ mmeaeɛ bi a asɛe (spot improvement), ɛkwan so aboseaguo (re-gravelling), ɛkwan so anweaguo (resealing), asefaateguo (asphaltic overlay), nsiesie kakra (partial reconstruction), atwene (bridges) ne dadeɛ anaa "sɛmɛnte- dorobɛn akɛseɛ a nsuo fa mu (culverts) wɔ akwan a ɛka nkuro akɛseɛ bɔ mu kwansini aduonu mmiɛnsa (23km), akwan a ɛkɔ baabi a mfudeɛ firi ba kwansini ahanwɔtwe (800km) ne kurom akwan kwansini ahasa (300).
Afei yɛbɛwie akwan a ɛka nkuro akɛseɛ bɔ mu kwansini aduɔson enum (75km), deɛ ɛkɔ baabi a mfudeɛ firi ba kwansini ɔha aduonum (150 km) ne kurom akwan kwansini aduasa (30 km) ho dwumadie.
Yɛgu so redi nnwuma atitire a ɛdidi soɔ yi na yɛbɛtoa so wɔ afe 2022 mu: Obetsebi Lamptey nsamsoɔ abɔnta no ɔfa a ɛtɔ so mmienu, Tema Motorway kanko no ɔfa ɛtɔ so mmienu no abɔnta nsamfoɔ a ɛtoa so mmiɛnsa no, La mpoano ɛkwan no ho dwumadie (deɛ ɛdi kan ne deɛ ɛtɔ so mmienu -Lots 1”2) Nkran-Mpoano ɛkwan no, ne Kumasi Tadeɛ ɛkwan ne Nsuka Ntrɛmu ho dwumadie.
Dwumadie a wɔnam so rehwɛ ama kuayɛ akɔ so wɔ afe no nyinaa mu no enti, yɛde sii yɛn ani so sɛ yɛbɛtu asutadeɛ dodoɔ ahanum aduosia (560 targeted dams) na yɛtumi tuu emu dodoɔ ahanum ne nson (507 dug out dams), na yɛagyina "Akuraase Baako Asutadeɛ Baako "nhyehyɛeɛ no so awie asutadeɛ afoforɔ aduɔwɔtwe (80 completed dams) bi nso.
Yɛgyinaa Kuayɛ ho dwumadie nhyehyɛeɛ no ɔfa a yɛfrɛ no IPEP no so wiee nkuraase edwa aduonum ne Mankassim edwa no ɔfa a ɛdi kan a suka a ne tenten yɛ mita ahanson aduɔwɔtwe ka ho bie no nyinaa.
Ɛsiane dwumadie a wɔnam so de rema ɔmanfoɔ a wɔwɔ ahoɔden anya adwuma ayɛ (Ghana Productive Safety Net - GPSNP) no enti, Aban gyinaa "Kuayɛ mu Nnɔbaeɛ a yɛde kɔ Amannɔne kɔtɔn ne Nkuraaseɛ Mpontuo Dwumadie (Planting for Export and Rural Development (PERD) Programme) no so kyekyɛɛ nnɔbaeɛ a yɛtɔn nya mu sika ho nnua nketewa dodoɔ ɔpepem nson ne mpem ahankron (7.9 million cash crop seedlings) maa akuafoɔ.
PERD mfudeɛ nnua nketewa a wɔde bɛma Kintampo akuafoɔ a wɔwɔ Bono Apueeɛ Mantam no
Aban gyinaa dwumadie a wɔato edin "Kuayɛ mu Nnɔbaeɛ a yɛde kɔ Amannɔne kɔtɔn ne Nkuraaseɛ Mpontuo- no so, kyekyɛɛ ateaa, kɔfe, kube, abɛ, mango, ne rɔba nnua nketewa a yɛde tintim dodoɔ ɔpepem baako mpem ahasa aduosia ne ahanum aduɔwɔtwe nkron, (1,360,589 cashew, coffee, coconut, oil palm, mango and rubber seedlings) memaa akuafoɔ dodoɔ mpem nson ahanum aduanan mmiɛnsa (7,543 farmers) a wɔwɔ Ahafo, Ashanti, Bono, mfinimfini, Apueeɛ, Nkran, Oti, Atɔeɛ, Atɔeɛ Atifi ne Firaw Amantam mu.
Aban wiee ntɔtemmoa ebuo edu baako a yɛde ani buu sɛ yɛbɛsi no mu edu ne nsubura dodoɔ dubaako maa ntɔtemmoayɛn mmeaeɛ ahodoɔ dubaako.

Ɛberɛ a amansan anammɔntuo wɔ COVID-19 yadeɛ kontonkurowi yi ano aduro ho wiee pɛyɔ no, paneɛwɔ nso bɛkaa akwanhodoɔ a na ɛwɔ hɔ dada a yɛde si yadeɛ no ano no ho.
Akwanhodoɔ yi a na adunom nka ho no ne nkatahwenehyɛ, nnipa asetenam nkutahodie mu ntetemu ɛnna nsa ho hohoro.
COVID-19 ho aduro a yɛde wɔ paneɛ a yɛfrɛ no AstraZeneca dodoɔ mpem ahansia (600,000 doses) a yɛde hyɛɛ aseɛ wɔ Ɔbɛnem bosome mu wɔ afe 2021 mu no, ɛduruu Obubuo bosome da a ɛtɔ so nwɔtwe no, na nnipa ɔpepem mmienu ne mpem ahanum (2.5 million persons) na awɔ wɔn paneɛ a ɛdi kan.
Seesei, nnipa a wɔadi boro mfeɛ dunwɔtwe a apemfoɔ nka ho bi no paneɛwɔ gu so rekɔ so wɔ mantam dunsia no nyinaa mu (all 16 regions) wɔ mansini ahanu ne aduosia baako (261 districts) mu wɔ ɔman yi afanan nyinaa.
Ɛsiane anammɔn a yɛretu de atrɛ ewiemhyɛn nketewa dwumadie (drone services) mu wɔ apɔmuden ho akadeɛ a ɛhia sɛ yɛde kɔ ɔman yi afan nyinaa no enti, Aban ne adwumakuo a yɛfrɛ no Zipline aka abom asisi mmeaeɛ a ewiemhyɛn nketewa yi ho dwumadie bɛkɔ so afoforɔ mmienu wɔ Anum wɔ Apueeɛ Mantam mu ɛnna Abujuro wɔ Oti Mantam mu.
Saa ewiemhyɛn nketewa mmeaeɛ mmienu yi bɛdi dwuma wɔ ayaresabea ahasa aduosia nsia na seesei dwumadie no rekɔ so a ayaresabea yi mu ɔha mu nkyɛmu aduonum ɛnan (54 %) na ayɛ den kakra sɛ ewiemhyɛn nketewa yi bɛtumi de apɔmuden ho akadeɛ akɔ hɔ.

Seesei, mansini ayaresabea aduɔkron baako ho beaeɛ ne adan ho mfoni (91 district hospital Cadastral plans) ayɛ krado dada.
Afe 2022 mu no, yɛbɛhyɛ aseɛ asisi 11 No. ayaresabea dodoɔ a mpa aduanan bɛkɔ mu (40-bed hospitals) ɛnna 1 No. ayaresabea dodoɔ a mpa aduasa bɛkɔ mu (30-bed hospitals) wɔ Asante, Bono, Nkran ne Apueeɛ Amantam mu.

Mpontunneɛ A Yɛde Bɛtu Ohia Ase Dwumadie yi yɛ dwumadie ahodoɔ a ɛdi akotene wɔ NPP Aban nhyehyɛeɛ ahodoɔ mu no baako a botaeɛ a ɛtaa akyire ne sɛ, yɛde mpontunneɛ bɛkɔ nkuraase, titire mpɔtam ahodoɔ a ohia wɔ hɔ no na ɛnam so aboa atu ohia ase na asetenam yiedie mu nsonsonoeɛ a ɛda nkuraasefoɔ ne kuromfoɔ ntam no asesa.
Yeinom yɛ susudua a wɔgyina so kyerɛ ɔman bi sikasɛm ho mmɔdenmmɔ.
Yei ho nhwɛsoɔ ne sikasɛm mpontuo, nneɛmaboɔ sorokɔ, adwumayɛ mu mpontuo mpɛmpɛnsoɔ, ɔman yi ne aman afoforɔ ntam edwadie mu mmɔdemmɔ, sɛdeɛ aban no sikasɛm ntotoeɛ kankorɔ anaa sintɔ teɛ ɛne sɛdeɛ gyinaeɛ ahodoɔ a Ghana Sikakorabea Baatan bɛsie no bɛka nneɛma boɔ sorokɔ wɔ ɔman no mu.
Nnwuma Nketewa yɛ ankorankorɛ nnwuma a wɔtumi fa nnipa kakra bi a dwetire a adwuma no gyina soɔ no mmoro sika dodoɔ pɔtee bi so.
Yei yɛ adwene foforɔ koraa a Ghana Aban de puee edwa a ɔnam so bɛma mmeranteɛ ne mmabaawa a wɔawie asuapɔn no bɛnya adwuma ayɛ wɔ ankorankorɛ anaa Aban nnwuma mu na wɔaboa de wɔn nimdeɛ apɛ asetenam ɔhaw ahodoɔ nnannuaguberɛ, na yei aboa wɔn ma wɔanya osuahunu a ɛbɛboa Ɔman sikasɛm mpontuo.
Ɔman Adwumakuo a Ɛhwɛ Mfidinnwuma Nketewa so no yɛ ɔman adwumakuo a ɛnyɛ mfasoɔ enti na wɔwɔ hɔ na mmom, ɛyɛ adwumakuo a ɛhyɛ Asoɛeɛ a Ɛhwɛ Edwadie ne Mfidinnwuma so no ase, a mmara ma wɔn ho ɛkwan sɛ wɔnhwɛ sɛ Mfidinnwuma Nketewa ne Adantam (Micro and Small Enterprises (MSEs) bɛtu mpɔn.
Yei yɛ ɔman yi mu adwadeɛ nyinaa bo ɛsom a sika a ɛfiri fango ho dwumadie ahodoɔ mu nka ho bi, a wɔagyina nneɛma boɔ sorokɔ so no asese.
Yei yɛ Aban sika a ɔnya a ɛmfiri ɛtoɔ mu.
Ebi ne sika a obi tua ansa na wanya tumi krataa bi adi dwuma ne sika a yɛtua wɔ nneɛma afoforɔ ho sɛ ebia nsiho ne mfantum ho.
Ɛka ahodoɔ a Aban de no yɛ sika a ɛsɛ sɛ Asoɛeɛ a Ɛhwɛ Sikasɛm so no tua ma Aban Nnwuma ahodoɔ no ɛnna afei, ɛka ɛda hɔ firi afe 2016 mu a mmara hyɛ sɛ ɛsɛ sɛ Aban tua gu Mmara ho Fotoɔ ahodoɔ mu.
Yei yɛ sika dodoɔ a aka wɔ ɛberɛ a Aban agyegye deɛ nqani da ɛkwan sɛ ɔbɛnya no nyinaa akyi de adi dwuma, a bosea ho nsiho nka ho bie, (kyerɛ sɛ bosea a Aban bɛtua nka ho).
Na ɛkwan a yɛfa so bu yei ho nkonta ne sɛ yɛsese no wɔ Ɔman Adwadeɛ nyinaa bo a ɛsom no mu ɔha mu nkyekyɛmu.
Yei yɛ sɛdeɛ ani da ɛkwan sɛ Ghana sikasɛm bɛtu mpɔn wɔ afe pɔtee bi mu.
Yei yɛ sika dodoɔ a Aban nya firi ne dwumadie
Ahodoɔ a ɔdi wɔ ɔman yi mu ne amannɔne.
Yei yɛ Ghana Aban mpontuo ho dwumadie foforɔ a ɔde aba a ɛnam so bɛma ntɔtemmoayɛn adwuma no adi mu na sika aba mu sɛdeɛ ɛbɛyɛ a, ntɔtemmoa nam bɛdɔɔso ama ɔmanfoɔ anya bi awe na yɛate ntɔtemmoa nam a yɛkra ba ɔman yi mu no so koraa, na ɛnam so ama nnwuma abu so a ɔmanfoɔ bɛnya bi ayɛ ama wɔn a wɔdi ntɔtemmoa ho dwuma nyinaa no asetenam nso
Yei yɛ sika a Aban gyina ɔmanfoɔ anan mu tua ma nsuo, pɛtro ne nkanea nnwumakuo no de sɔ
Yei yɛ sika mu mmoa bi a Aban de ma ɔman mma ma ɛyi adesoa kakra firi wɔn so ma wɔn kɔn mu dwo wɔn.
Yei yɛ ɛkwan a yɛfa so de ani di biribi
Akyi na yɛhwɛ sɛdeɛ ɛsi teɛ.
Yei ne sɛ Aban bɛma ɔman mma ahunu deɛ ɛrekɔ
So na wɔate amanneɛbɔ nyinaa wɔ ɛberɛ ano ɛberɛ ano.
Yei yɛ sika dodoɔ a Aban tua wɔ dwuma a ɔdi ne dwumadie a ɔdi ma ɔmanfoɔ ho ho.
Yei yɛ ɛtoɔ bi a yɛde to adwadeɛ ahodoɔ so wɔ adwadeɛyɛ mpɛmpɛnsoɔ biara mu.
